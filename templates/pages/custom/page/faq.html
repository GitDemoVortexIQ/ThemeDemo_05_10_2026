sidebar:
  • Are you an EU customer?

    As of the 1st January 2021, our products can be ordered via our website and delivered to EU countries - our prices will be UK VAT free for EU customers.

    Products delivered to the EU may be subject to local import taxes when they reach your country’s border. The receiver of the order, not the person placing the order, will typically receive a separate request for payment of local import taxes when the goods arrive in the country of destination, from their own country’s Customs and Excise office. If you are ordering products to be delivered to someone else, please ensure they are aware that they’ll be responsible for paying these additional import taxes.

    If the receiver wishes to return the product, they must request a refund of the paid Customs duties and tax directly with their local Customs office and must provide proof of return.

    We look forward to continuing to serve all our EU customers.

  • You a UK customer?

    If you are ordering within the UK, prices will be displayed including and excluding VAT. Where you have requested delivery to a UK address, the price paid for the product and applicable delivery charges will include UK VAT.

  • Additional Shipping Charges?

    On rare occasions, extra shipping charges may apply when delivering to non-UK mainland and remote areas, and our Sales team will contact you on receipt of your order.

  • Orders?

    All orders are subject to acceptance and availability by us (Wood Workers Workshop Limited). If the goods ordered are not available, you will be notified by e-mail or phone straightaway and you will have the option either to wait until the item is available from stock or to cancel your order.

    Any orders placed by you will be treated as an offer to purchase the goods or services from us and we have the right to reject such offers. Any automated order confirmation which you receive does not amount to our acceptance of your order, and conclusion of the contract between us takes place when we (a) debit your credit/debit card or (b) dispatch the goods to you or commence the services, whichever is the latter.

    We accept payment by Visa, MasterCard, Amex and Diners Club credit cards, Visa Debit cards, PayPal and Bank Transfer. We do not accept payment by pre-paid Credit Payment Cards or cheques.

    To view your previous orders, sign into your account and then choose an order from your 'Order History'.

    The products sold on the Wood Workers Workshop website are not for re-sale or distribution.

    Please refer to our Terms and Conditions for further information.

  • Gift Cards?

    You can buy gift cards and they are delivered electronically to the purchaser’s email address.

    Our gift cards remain valid for 12 months.

    Shop as usual on the website and then proceed to checkout. During checkout you’ll be asked to enter the unique code for each gift card you wish to use. After entering the gift card codes, your order balance will automatically be updated with credit to the value of the gift card(s). If there’s any balance to pay, you can use a debit or credit card in the usual way.

    Any partial balance remaining on the gift card can be reviewed at any time by accessing the gift card button on the original delivery email.

    We cannot exchange gift cards for cash.

    Gift cards cannot be used for telephone orders and the unique code can only be used on the website.
