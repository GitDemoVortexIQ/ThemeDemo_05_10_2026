Looking for a gift or shopping for someone else but not sure what to give them? Give them the gift of choice with a Wood Workers Workshop e-Gift Card, ranging from £10 to £350.

e-Gift cards are sent immediately by email to either the buyer or directly to the recipient and are redeemable online at woodworkersworkshop.co.uk. To find out how to redeem an e-Gift Card click on the Redeem e-Gift Cards link below. e-Gift Cards can only be purchased and redeemed in the UK.

To keep track of how much is on your e-Gift Card click on the Check e-Gift Cards Balance link below. The amount of a purchase will be deducted from the balance of the card. Any remaining balance on an e-Gift Card can be used as full or part payment on a future purchase.

e-Gift Cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Any remaining balance left after the expiry date will be cancelled and the e-Gift Card will become invalid and no longer available for use.
